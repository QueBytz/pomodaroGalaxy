Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Cosmic Pomodoro.

What is the Pomodoro Technique? +
The Pomodoro Technique is a proven time-management method created by Francesco Cirillo. It uses focused work intervals (usually 25 minutes), followed by short restorative breaks (5 minutes). After four intervals, you take a longer break (15–30 minutes) to prevent cognitive burnout.
Does the timer keep running if I switch apps or lock my screen? +
Yes! Cosmic Pomodoro uses an integrated Android Foreground Service. It maintains an ongoing countdown in your notification shade and calculates elapsed wall-clock time so the timer never pauses or desynchronizes when your phone is locked.
Is an internet connection or user account required? +
Not at all. Cosmic Pomodoro is 100% offline-first. There are no user sign-ups, no logins, and all session notes and history are saved strictly on your local device.
Can I customize the session and break durations? +
Yes. Open the in-app Cosmic Settings dialog to configure your preferred Focus duration (1 to 90 minutes), Short Break, and Long Break durations to match your personal study schedule.
