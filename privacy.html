Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Summary for Users: Cosmic Pomodoro is an offline-first focus application developed by Quebytz. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information, location data, or usage metrics. Your focus history and notes remain 100% on your device.

01. Introduction

Cosmic Pomodoro ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning information collection, usage, and disclosure when you use the Cosmic Pomodoro mobile and web applications.

By using Cosmic Pomodoro, you agree to the policies described in this document. We are committed to maintaining a distraction-free and privacy-respecting environment for all focused minds.

02. Information We Do NOT Collect

Cosmic Pomodoro operates without requiring personal data of any kind. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Location Data: We do not access GPS, IP geolocations, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.
  • No Device Identifiers or Telemetry: We do not collect IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), or device fingerprints.
  • No Usage or Behavioral Analytics: There are no third-party tracking cookies or behavioral tracking frameworks installed in this app.

03. Local Storage & Data Ownership

All session logs, timer configurations, and personal focus reflections created inside the application are stored solely in your device’s local private sandbox storage:

  • Session Records: Duration of sessions, categorized tags (e.g. Study, Work, Coding), timestamps, and optional user notes are stored in local JSON files.
  • User Settings: Custom session minutes, sound/haptic preferences, and Do Not Disturb toggles are preserved locally.

This data is never uploaded to remote cloud databases, external servers, or accessible to Quebytz. You have absolute ownership and control of your productivity records.

04. Application Permissions Explained

To provide a reliable timer experience while minimizing power usage, Cosmic Pomodoro requests the following standard Android permissions:

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing countdown timer and session completion alerts in the system status bar.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
SPECIAL_USE		 Allows the timer service to continue calculating time reliably when you switch apps or minimize the screen without being terminated by battery management.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback during button clicks, time-warp scrubbing, and the supernova completion animation.
WAKE_LOCK Used optionally when the "Keep Screen Awake" preference is turned on in Cosmic Settings to prevent display sleep during focus periods.

05. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Cosmic Pomodoro does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob or Unity Ads), social media trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics).

06. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. Because the application does not collect any personally identifiable information, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

07. Data Deletion & User Rights

You can delete your data at any time directly within the application:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the History dialog inside the app to delete specific session records or clear your entire focus history in one click.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Cosmic Pomodoro from your device immediately and permanently deletes all local storage files associated with the app.

08. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any updates will be published to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
